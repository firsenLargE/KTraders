# How Kapil Traders Calculates Your Business Data

This guide explains how the system calculates your profits, stock values, and shopping lists in simple terms.

---

## 1. Taxes (VAT)
The system automatically adds the government-mandated **13% VAT** to your sales.
*   **Simple Math:** If an item costs ₹100, the VAT is ₹13, and the total is ₹113.
*   **Why it matters:** You always see the correct "final price" for your customers and total tax collected.

---

## 2. Product "Importance" Ranking
Your dashboard highlights certain products. The system "scores" products to see what deserves your attention.
*   **How it works:** A product gets a higher score if it's from a premium brand (like *Bottlers Nepal*) or if you have a lot of it in stock.
*   **Why it matters:** It helps you identify your most valuable inventory at a glance.

---

## 3. Profit & Loss: How much are you making?
The system calculates your profit by taking your **Total Sales** and subtracting the **Cost of Buying** the items.
*   **How it finds the cost:** If you haven't manually entered a cost price, the system looks at your **latest purchase bills**. If those are missing, it assumes you're making roughly a **20% margin** as a safe guess.
*   **Why it matters:** You get a realistic look at your earnings without having to do manual math for every sale.

---

## 4. AI Predictions: Guessing Future Sales
The "AI Forecasting" looks at your last 12 months of sales to predict the next 3 months.
*   **How it works:** It looks for **Trends** (is the product getting more popular?) and **Seasonality** (do you sell more Beer in Summer?).
*   **Why it matters:** It helps you prepare for busy months before they happen so you never run out of stock.

---

## 5. Smart Shopping List (Budget Planner)
The Budget Planner acts like a personal assistant for your shopping.
*   **How it works:** It looks at your current budget and picks items that are:
    1.  Below their minimum stock level.
    2.  From high-demand brands.
    3.  Affordable within your budget.
*   **Goal:** To give you the "healthiest" inventory possible for every rupee you spend.

---

## 6. Stock Alerts
*   **Low Stock:** Every product has a "Safe Zone" (Minimum Stock). If you fall below this, the system highlights it in Red.
*   **Net Stock:** Adding a purchase bill automatically increases your stock; making a sale decreases it. No manual counting needed!
